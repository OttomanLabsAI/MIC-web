Who we are

Faith and construction meet in the same place

Compass, straightedge, repetition — the geometry on these pages is the geometry of the buildings we work on. The idea behind MIC is just as plain: Muslim professionals across UK construction spend the year on separate projects, so we build the rooms where they meet.

  • 01The networkA UK-wide WhatsApp community where members, questions and opportunities find each other — free, and open to students as much as directors.
  • 02The gala & awardsOne night a year in London: a halal sit-down dinner, keynotes from people who have done the work, and awards for those moving the industry forward.
  • 03The treksDays on the hills that double as fundraisers — the next one funds schools for children through READ Foundation.
  • 04The open doorMost people come to their first MIC event alone. The formats are built so nobody leaves a stranger.
