Five hours, set out the way you'd set out a programme. Arrive at six, eat at seven, awards at nine, home by eleven.

  1. +18:00

    Doors and registration

    Arrival, name badges, and the first coffee of a long evening.

  2. +18:20

    Welcome reception

    Open networking. Bring cards — this is the hour most people say they got the most out of.

  3. +19:00

    Guests seated · opening

    Recitation and a welcome from the MIC committee.

  4. +19:15

    Starter served

    Three-course halal menu begins. Dietary requirements taken at booking.

    Speaker announced September. Twenty minutes, one career, no slides.

MIC Awards 2026

Eight awards. Nominations open September.

The MIC Awards recognise people making a measurable difference across construction and the built environment — on site, in the office, and in the community around both.

01

Rising Star

Under five years in the industry, already changing how their team works.

02

Engineer of the Year

Technical excellence across civil, structural, MEP or geotechnical.

03

Site Leadership

Site and project managers who deliver without burning out their people.

04

Woman in Construction

Leadership and impact in a sector still learning to make room.

05

Innovation

Digital, BIM, MMC or sustainability work that others have since copied.

06

Community Impact

Work beyond the day job — mentoring, outreach, charity build projects.

07

Employer of the Year

Firms that back their Muslim staff in practice, not just in policy.

08

Student of the Year

Undergraduates and apprentices already contributing to the industry.

Before you book

Questions we get asked

Is the food halal?

Yes. The full three-course menu is halal and the venue is alcohol-free. Vegetarian, vegan, nut-free and other dietary requirements are captured at checkout and confirmed with the caterer two weeks before.

Is there a prayer room?

Yes, a prayer space is available throughout the evening with wudu facilities. Isha will have passed before doors at 18:00 on 28 November, but the room stays open regardless.

What should I wear?

Formal or black tie. Suits, thobes, sherwanis and modest formal wear are all in keeping — the room is dressed up, and dressed modestly.

What are the seating arrangements?

Both mixed and separate seating areas are available. You choose your preference at booking and we seat accordingly.

Can I book a table for my company?

Yes. A table of ten is £520, which works out cheaper per head than individual seats, and puts your team together with your company name on the table card. We can invoice your firm — email us and we'll raise it.

I'm coming alone — will I know anyone?

Most people arrive alone. Solo bookings are seated deliberately, not randomly: tell us your discipline at checkout and we'll put you with people worth meeting.

Can I get a refund?

Tickets are non-refundable but fully transferable to a colleague up to 7 days before the event. Email us with the new attendee's details and we'll reissue.

How do I nominate someone for an award?

Nominations open in September. Add your email above and we'll send you the form the day it goes live — self-nomination is allowed and encouraged.
