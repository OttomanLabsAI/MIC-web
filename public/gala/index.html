The evening

Not a networking event with dinner attached

The MIC Gala is the flagship night of the Muslims in Construction calendar. It brings together engineers, quantity surveyors, site managers, architects, developers and students from across the UK — people who spend the year on separate projects and rarely end up in the same room.

The format is deliberate: a proper sit-down dinner so conversations last longer than a business card, keynotes from people who have actually done the work, and awards that name the individuals and employers moving the industry forward.

  • 01Three-course dinnerFully halal, served at the table. Dietary requirements captured at booking.
  • 02KeynotesSenior figures from across the built environment. Speakers TBA
  • 03The MIC Awards 2026Recognising individuals, teams and employers making a measurable difference.
  • 04Structured networkingSeating planned by discipline and career stage, not left to chance.
  • 05Prayer facilitiesA quiet prayer space is open throughout. Isha will have passed before doors.

Before you book

Questions people actually ask

Is the food halal?

Yes. The full menu is halal and the event is alcohol-free throughout. Vegetarian, vegan and allergen-specific options are available — you tell us at booking and we pass it to the venue. Confirm certification

What are the seating arrangements?

Round tables of ten. Individual bookings are seated by discipline and career stage where we can, so you sit near people worth talking to. Confirm mixed / separate seating policy

Is there somewhere to pray?

Yes — a quiet prayer space is available throughout the evening. Isha will already have passed before doors open at 18:00 in late November, so no break is scheduled into the programme.

What does black tie actually mean here?

Formal evening wear. A dark suit and tie is perfectly fine; a dinner jacket is welcome. Traditional formal dress is equally welcome.

Can I book a table for my company and confirm names later?

Yes. Reserve the table now and send the attendee names up to two weeks before the event. We can invoice your company rather than take card payment. Confirm invoicing terms

Can I get a refund or transfer my ticket?

Tickets are transferable to another person free of charge up to seven days before the event — just send us the new name. Refunds are available up to date TBC, after which catering is committed. Confirm policy

Is the venue step-free?

To confirm with venue. If you have access requirements, email us and we will arrange seating and arrival around them.

I have never been to an MIC event. Will I know anyone?

Most people arrive alone. That is the point of the seating plan — you will be sat with people in your discipline, and the reception before dinner is built for exactly this. Come on your own; you will not leave on your own.
